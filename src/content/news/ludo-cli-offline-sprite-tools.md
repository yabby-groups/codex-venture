<p lang="en">For a 2D game prototype, Ludo CLI can process sprites you already have using its offline tools. Ludo AI lists 32 free offline tools in the repository, including sprite sheet processing, pivot and collider tools, and export options for Godot, Unity, and Phaser. The repository lists Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Gemini CLI among the supported coding agents.</p>

<p lang="en">Start with the export option for your target engine, then pick the sprite sheet, pivot, or collider tools your handoff requires.
