<h2><span lang="zh-CN">项目用途</span><span lang="en">What it does</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">Handy 把录音、离线转写和粘贴连在一个快捷键操作中，可把口述内容送入当前编辑器。</p>

<p lang="en">Handy combines recording, offline transcription and pasting into one hotkey flow, putting dictated text into the current editor.</p>

<h2><span lang="zh-CN">适用场景</span><span lang="en">Where to use it</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">适合写短笔记或草拟消息。标点、术语和口音都会影响输出，常用领域词汇可作为选择识别模型时的测试样本。</p>

<p lang="en">It suits short notes and message drafts. Punctuation, terminology and accents affect the output. Use frequently spoken domain terms when testing recognition models.</p>
