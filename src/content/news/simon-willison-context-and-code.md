<p lang="zh-CN">Simon Willison 在 2025 年 3 月的文章里回顾了两年多使用大模型辅助编程的经验。他常直接使用 ChatGPT 与 Claude 的网页或应用界面，这样便于明确自己提供了哪些上下文。对于复杂任务，他会先让模型写出简单版本，验证后再继续扩展。</p>

<p lang="en">In March 2025, Simon Willison described more than two years of using language models for coding. He often worked directly with the ChatGPT and Claude web or app interfaces so he could see what context he supplied. For complex work he asked for a simple version, checked it, and iterated from there.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">作者指出模型会编造库和方法，旧训练资料也会拖累新版接口的使用。他的做法是提供现有代码、完整示例或版本资料。文中所说的良好体验属于作者自述，缺少可独立比较的成本与生产率数据。团队可借鉴的是先定义一个能检查的中间结果。</p>

<p lang="en">Willison describes hallucinated libraries and methods, and how stale training data can undermine work with newer APIs. He responds by supplying existing code, complete examples, or version-specific material. The positive experience is his own account, with no comparable cost or productivity figures. Teams can borrow the practice of defining a verifiable intermediate result.</p>
