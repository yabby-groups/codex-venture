<p lang="zh-CN">独立开发者一边交付项目、一边找客户时，每次重新打开邮件确认上次聊到哪里，都会拖慢跟进。Hermit CRM 1.2.0 的官网给出了一种组织办法：用公司时间线集中记录邮件、电话和会议，用任务记下下一步，再从销售看板找出尚未收到回复的沟通。公司、联系人、任务和外联记录都以纯文本文件保存在自己的电脑上。<a href="https://hermitcrm.io/">Hermit CRM 官网</a></p>

<p lang="en">When you handle both delivery and sales, follow-ups slow down if you have to reopen old emails to remember where each conversation left off. The Hermit CRM 1.2.0 site describes a way to organize that work: keep emails, calls, and meetings on a company timeline, record the next action as a task, and use the pipeline board to find unanswered conversations. Company, contact, task, and outreach records are stored as plain-text files on your computer. <a href="https://hermitcrm.io/">Hermit CRM website</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">按这套设计，可以先从看板选出待跟进的公司，再打开时间线核对最后一次沟通和约定的下一步。官网还介绍了外联功能：它可以研究公司网站，起草首封消息或跟进消息，然后等待用户批准。草稿审核时，可以把其中引用的事实和承诺与公司记录逐项对照。</p>

<p lang="en">In this workflow, you can pick a company from the board, then open its timeline to check the last exchange and the agreed next step. The site also describes an outreach feature that researches a company website, drafts an initial or follow-up message, and waits for approval. During that review, you can check the draft’s facts and commitments against the company record.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">官网列出了 Mac 和 Linux 下载，并标注 Apache 2.0 许可。页面上的功能截图使用演示数据。本文基于项目官网，未运行应用，两周未跟进之类的代理查询仍需在实际环境中验证。</p>

<p lang="en">The site lists downloads for Mac and Linux under the Apache 2.0 license. Its feature screenshots use demo data. This article is based on the project site and did not run the app. An agent query such as finding contacts who have gone two weeks without a follow-up still needs verification in a working setup.</p>
