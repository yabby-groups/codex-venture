<p lang="zh-CN">冬小麦的施氮计划可能因天气、农机调度或预算变化而需要在生长季内调整。Xulong Wang 等九位作者在 11 月 23 日提交的预印本中介绍了一套面向英格兰冬小麦的两阶段方法。系统先用机器学习估计的氮肥响应曲线制定基准计划，再用大语言模型根据种植者面临的约束调整当季建议。</p>

<p lang="en">Weather, equipment availability, and budget changes can force winter wheat growers to revise their nitrogen plans midseason. In a preprint submitted on November 23, Xulong Wang and eight coauthors describe a two-stage approach for winter wheat in England. It uses a nitrogen-response curve estimated with machine learning to set a baseline plan, then uses a large language model to adapt recommendations to the grower’s constraints.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">作者报告，在田间试验和农场试验中，基准方案用更少的氮肥达到了常规做法的产量水平。经过季中调整的方案还使谷物达到质量标准。试验结果对应英格兰冬小麦，具体减氮幅度未在本批提供的摘要中量化。</p>

<p lang="en">The authors report that in field and farm trials, the baseline schedules matched standard-practice yields with less nitrogen. The schedules adjusted during the season also met grain quality standards. The trials covered winter wheat in England, and the available abstract does not quantify the nitrogen savings.</p>
