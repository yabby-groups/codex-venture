<p lang="zh-CN">OwnShop AI 的蜂蜜订单演示停在一个关键的人工操作上。顾客在聊天里查库存、选数量、回复确认订单，随后收到带金额和订单号的 UPI 二维码。店主点击确认收款后，演示才显示回执。<a href="https://ownshopai.store/">OwnShop AI 官网</a></p>

<p lang="en">OwnShop AI’s honey-order demo hinges on one action by the shopkeeper. The customer checks stock in chat, picks a quantity, confirms the order, and receives a UPI QR code with the amount and order number. The demo shows a receipt after the shopkeeper marks the payment as received. <a href="https://ownshopai.store/">OwnShop AI website</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">店主可以先在内置网页聊天里用几行虚构商品试走一遍。上传带价格和库存的 Excel 表，核对聊天回复与表格是否一致，再检查订单金额、付款提示和人工确认点。确认流程合适后，再考虑接入 WhatsApp 或 Telegram。官网列出的安装包约 700 MB，支持 Windows x64、Windows ARM64 和 Apple 芯片 Mac。这里的流程来自官方演示，实际接单效果需要用自家表格和业务规则验证。</p>

<p lang="en">Shopkeepers can start with a few fictitious products in the built-in web chat. Upload an Excel sheet with prices and stock counts, check the replies against the sheet, then review the order total, payment instructions, and manual confirmation step. Once the flow fits, consider connecting WhatsApp or Telegram. The website lists an approximately 700 MB download for Windows x64, Windows ARM64, and Apple silicon Macs. The flow comes from the official demo. Results need to be checked against the shop’s own spreadsheet and business rules.</p>
