<p lang="zh-CN">OpenAI 在 11 月 19 日更新了 Businesses in ChatGPT 的测试版公告，将 Business Agent 发布资格扩展到美国以外符合条件的电商和在线服务商家。11 月 10 日首次公布时，Agent 面向的是美国符合条件的同类商家。</p>

<p lang="en">OpenAI updated its Businesses in ChatGPT beta announcement on November 19, extending Business Agent publishing to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses outside the United States. When OpenAI introduced the beta on November 10, agent publishing initially covered eligible businesses in those categories in the U.S.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">商家可以通过 Business Hub 认领企业资料，验证后更新关键信息，并自行提交含价格和商品可售状态的数据源。OpenAI 也将互动洞察列为 Business Hub 的功能，供商家了解用户在 ChatGPT 中如何与企业互动。</p>

<p lang="en">Businesses can claim their profile through Business Hub. Once verified, they can update key details and submit a self-service product feed with prices and availability. OpenAI also lists engagement insights in Business Hub so businesses can see how people interact with them in ChatGPT.</p>
