<p lang="zh-CN">GitHub 11 月 25 日宣布，这次调整适用于使用 Copilot Business 或 Copilot Enterprise、尚未保存相关计费选项的组织和企业。从 2026 年 12 月 30 日起，持有付费 Copilot 许可证的成员发起的 Copilot 代码审查，默认由仓库所属组织付费。未启用 AI Credits 付费用量、存在计费锁定或没有可计费客户的账户，设置将改为 Member。</p>

<p lang="en">GitHub announced on November 25 that the change applies to organizations and enterprises on Copilot Business or Copilot Enterprise that have yet to save a billing choice for these reviews. Starting December 30, 2026, Copilot code reviews requested by members with a paid Copilot license will be billed to the organization that owns the repository by default. Accounts with AI Credits paid usage disabled, a billing lock, or no billable customer will be set to Member.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">组织计费需要启用 AI Credits 付费用量，可能增加组织支出。想继续使用成员个人的 Copilot 额度，可在生效前到组织或企业的 Copilot 策略中选择 Member。如果该策略由企业统一管理，则由企业所有者设置。</p>

<p lang="en">Organization billing requires AI Credits paid usage and can increase the organization’s spending. To keep using members’ individual Copilot entitlements, select Member in the organization or enterprise Copilot policy before the change takes effect. If the enterprise manages the policy, an enterprise owner makes that choice.</p>
