<h2><span lang="zh-CN">主要变化</span><span lang="en">What changed</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">Google 11 月 20 日介绍了 Watch Duty 将应急无线电转换成文本、识别野火出警的应用。</p>

<p lang="en">On November 20, Google described how Watch Duty transcribes emergency radio and identifies wildfire dispatches.</p>

<h2><span lang="zh-CN">实际用途</span><span lang="en">Practical use</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">广播里的地址、单位代号和重复呼叫都可能影响后续分流。对类似应用，报告从原始无线电到人工确认的用时，比单报转录速度更能反映工作流。</p>

<p lang="en">Addresses, unit identifiers and repeated calls can all affect dispatch triage. For similar systems, timing the path from radio audio to human confirmation describes the workflow better than transcription speed alone.</p>

<h2><span lang="zh-CN">获取与范围</span><span lang="en">Availability and scope</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">读者可在 Google 案例中查看 Watch Duty 的流程。它是合作方报道，部署到其他地区前需用当地广播、地名和术语做验证。</p>

<p lang="en">Google’s case study describes the Watch Duty workflow. This is a partner account, and other regions need validation with their own radio feeds, place names and terminology.</p>
