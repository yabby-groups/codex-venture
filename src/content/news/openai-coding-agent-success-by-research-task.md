<p lang="zh-CN">OpenAI 在 2026 年 11 月 18 日公布的内部评估中报告，编码代理自主监看训练运行的成功率为 79%，启动训练运行为 35%，编写推理与服务栈代码为 25%。对正在给代理派工的团队来说，同一份报告里的这三项结果表明，任务类型值得单独记录。<a href="https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier">OpenAI 研究报告</a></p>

<p lang="en">In an internal assessment published on November 18, 2026, OpenAI reported autonomous coding-agent success rates of 79% for monitoring training runs, 35% for launching them, and 25% for writing inference and serving code. For teams assigning work to agents, the spread within a single report makes a case for tracking results by task type. <a href="https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier">OpenAI research report</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">从 3 月到 10 月，预计需要人工两至四小时的研究任务，自主成功率估计值从 23% 升到 46%。预计需要人工四至八小时的任务从 13% 升到 35%。团队可以从结果容易核验的监控任务开始试点，按任务类型分别记录自主完成和人工接手的情况，再用自己的数据决定是否扩大委派范围。</p>

<p lang="en">From March to October, estimated autonomous success rose from 23% to 46% on research tasks expected to take a person two to four hours, and from 13% to 35% on tasks expected to take four to eight hours. Teams can start with monitoring tasks whose results are easy to verify, log autonomous completions and human takeovers by task type, and use their own data to decide whether to delegate more.</p>
