<p lang="zh-CN">OpenAI 在 2026 年 11 月 17 日披露，北约核心机构已通过北约通信和信息局（NCIA）获得 OpenAI 的网络安全模型访问权限。这些模型通过 Daybreak 用于获授权的防御工作，包括审查软件、调查可疑活动和测试修复方案。</p>

<p lang="en">On November 17, 2026, OpenAI disclosed that NATO’s core institutions had gained access to its cybersecurity models through the NATO Communications and Information Agency (NCIA). The models are available through Daybreak for authorized defensive work, including software review, investigation of suspicious activity, and testing fixes.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">OpenAI 还表示，北约在这项合作中的单月用量最高达到 3000 亿原始 token，合计输入与输出。2026 年 Crossed Swords 演习也在新增的训练环节使用了 Daybreak，演习强调由人员监督 AI 智能体。</p>

<p lang="en">OpenAI also reported that NATO’s usage in the partnership reached up to 300 billion raw tokens in a single month, counting both input and output. The 2026 Crossed Swords exercise also used Daybreak in a new training stream that emphasized human oversight of AI agents.</p>
