<p lang="zh-CN">想知道新用户能否完成首次设置，项目作者可以交给 Voom 一个链接、一项任务和最多三个问题。按 Voom 的官方文档，助手可以提交请求并取回反馈，试用者在自己的浏览器里完成任务，再用自己的话写下卡住的地方。<a href="https://voom.ink/agents">Voom 文档</a></p>

<p lang="en">To find out whether new users can complete setup, a maker can give Voom a link, one task, and up to three questions. According to Voom’s documentation, an assistant can submit the request and retrieve the feedback. Testers try the task in their own browsers and describe where they got stuck in their own words. <a href="https://voom.ink/agents">Voom documentation</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">这套设计用印章交换试用时间。每人开始有两枚印章，发起一次面向三名试用者的请求需要两枚。一条反馈被项目作者接受后，撰写者获得一枚。助手代为接受或举报反馈前，需要先征询用户。</p>

<p lang="en">The design uses stamps to exchange testing time. Everyone starts with two stamps, and a request for three testers costs two. A tester earns one stamp when the maker accepts their note. The assistant asks the user before accepting or reporting feedback on their behalf.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">一次请求围绕一项具体任务组织，收到的反馈可以作为排查操作障碍的线索。现有文档摘录只说明了交换规则和人工确认环节，实际等待时间、完成率和反馈质量仍待验证。</p>

<p lang="en">Each request centers on a specific task, and the resulting notes can offer clues about where users run into trouble. The supplied documentation describes the exchange and its approval steps. Actual turnaround, completion rates, and feedback quality remain unverified.</p>
