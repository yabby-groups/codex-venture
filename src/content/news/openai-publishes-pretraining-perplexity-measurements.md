<p lang="zh-CN">11 月 22 日，OpenAI 首席科学家 Jakub Pachocki 在署名文章中公开了 OpenAI 内部的困惑度测量结果。困惑度反映模型预测下一个词元时的不确定程度。在相同评估条件下，困惑度越低，表示模型对评估文本的预测越准确。<a href="https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence">OpenAI 原文</a></p>

<p lang="en">On November 22, OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published the company’s internal perplexity measurements in a signed article. Perplexity reflects how uncertain a model is when predicting the next token. Under the same evaluation conditions, lower perplexity indicates better predictions on the evaluation text. <a href="https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence">OpenAI’s original article</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">Pachocki 提议用困惑度或与之对应的交叉熵损失来跟踪预训练进展，为不同模型开发者提供可比较的指标，并为制定共同的安全标准提供参考。OpenAI 此次公开的是自家的测量结果。要比较其他开发者的结果，还需要可比的评估条件。</p>

<p lang="en">Pachocki proposed using perplexity or its corresponding cross-entropy loss to track pretraining progress, compare results across model developers, and inform shared safety standards. The disclosure provides measurements from OpenAI. Comparing them with results from other developers would require comparable evaluation conditions.</p>
