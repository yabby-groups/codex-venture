<p lang="zh-CN">UFO OPTIX 为公开卫星影像中的飞机目击记录链接原始来源，并用状态标签标明核对进展。一条新西兰布莱尼姆附近的记录附有 ADS-B 匹配。按项目公布的数据，航班记录位置距影像检测位置约 41 米，匹配机型为 ATR 72-600。</p>

<p lang="en">UFO OPTIX links aircraft sightings in public satellite imagery to their source images and labels how far each sighting has been checked. One entry near Blenheim, New Zealand, includes an ADS-B match. The project reports that the flight record for an ATR 72-600 placed it about 41 metres from the detection in the image.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">同一条记录分别列出影像估算的约 313 节表观速度和航班记录报告的 239 节地速。阿尔及利亚德利斯以北的另一条记录通过了图像复核，但没有匹配到航班记录，项目将其标为未识别。两个案例展示了页面如何记录各自的证据，也说明项目摘录所能支持的范围仅限于这些个案。<a href="https://ufoptix.org/">UFO OPTIX 原始记录</a></p>

<p lang="en">The same entry lists an apparent speed of about 313 knots estimated from the image and a reported ground speed of 239 knots from the flight record. Another entry, north of Dellys, Algeria, passed image review and remains unidentified because it has no flight-record match. These cases show how the page records the evidence for each sighting. The excerpt covers individual cases and provides no measure of overall detection accuracy. <a href="https://ufoptix.org/">UFO OPTIX records</a></p>
