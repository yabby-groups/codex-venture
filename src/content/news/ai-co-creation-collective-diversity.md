<p lang="zh-CN">编辑团队试用 AI 时，可以为单篇稿件打分，也可以把整批稿件排在一起看选题有多少重合。11 月 26 日 arXiv 收录的 v1 预印本通过众包研究考察了创意假设生成和故事写作。作者报告，AI 协作可以提高单件作品质量并减少投入，但随着采用比例上升，作品整体的多样性下降。实验中，更细粒度的人机协作和更多样的 Agent 策略缓解了这一下降。</p>

<p lang="en">When an editorial team trials AI, it can score individual drafts and also lay them side by side to see how often they cover the same ideas. A v1 preprint listed by arXiv on November 26 used a crowdsourcing study to examine creative hypothesis generation and story writing. The authors report that AI assistance could improve individual works and reduce effort, while diversity across the collection declined as adoption rose. More fine-grained collaboration and a wider range of agent strategies moderated that decline in the experiments.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">编辑室可以用同一题目做一次小试验。一组让 AI 起草，另一组先各自定下立意，再分步使用 AI。用同一套标准评单篇质量，另记整批稿件中重复出现的主题或叙事角度。这项对照可以检验两套流程在本团队里的整体表现。若要单独判断哪一步影响了重复度，可以在下一轮只改一个环节。<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33192">arXiv 预印本</a></p>

<p lang="en">An editorial team can run a small trial with the same brief. Give one group AI-generated first drafts and have the other choose their own angles before using AI in stages. Score individual drafts using the same rubric, then separately log repeated themes or story angles across each batch. This compares the workflows as a whole in that team. A follow-up that changes one step at a time can help identify what drives overlap. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33192">arXiv preprint</a></p>
