<p lang="zh-CN">手边只有 Android 手机或平板、又想继续改代码时，可以先看 NativeCode 的设备要求。zenithblue-oss 在项目 README 中将 NativeCode 描述为一套可在 Android 设备上运行 Debian 桌面和 AI 编码代理的开发环境。</p>

<p lang="en">If an Android phone or tablet is the only device you have on hand and you want to keep coding, start by checking NativeCode’s device requirements. In the project README, zenithblue-oss describes NativeCode as a development environment that runs a Debian desktop and AI coding agents on Android.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">Android 8.0 或更高版本和至少 10 GB 的空闲内部存储是硬性要求。README 另建议配备至少 7 GB 内存和较新的处理器，列出的例子包括骁龙 8 Gen 2、天玑 9200、Tensor G3 和 Exynos 2200。项目 README 标注许可证为 GPL-3.0-or-later。<a href="https://github.com/zenithblue-oss/nativecode">NativeCode 官方仓库</a></p>

<p lang="en">Android 8.0 or later and at least 10 GB of free internal storage are mandatory. The README separately recommends at least 7 GB of RAM and a recent processor, with examples including the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, Dimensity 9200, Tensor G3, and Exynos 2200. The project README lists GPL-3.0-or-later as the license. <a href="https://github.com/zenithblue-oss/nativecode">Official NativeCode repository</a></p>
