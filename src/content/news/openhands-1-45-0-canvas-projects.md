<p lang="zh-CN">OpenHands 于 2026 年 11 月 24 日发布 v1.45.0，在 Canvas 中加入实验性的 Projects 功能。发布说明将这项功能和项目界面的布局调整归功于 @enyst。用户可以从侧栏切换项目会话，并在聊天旁查看文件和拉取请求。</p>

<p lang="en">OpenHands released v1.45.0 on November 24, 2026, adding experimental Projects support to Canvas. The release notes credit @enyst with the feature and its layout changes. Users can switch between project conversations in the sidebar and view files and pull requests alongside the chat.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">由 @neubig 贡献的界面改动将子代理会话归到对应的父会话下，方便用户追踪任务委派后的对话。@jpshackelford 贡献的 Factory 页面改动让用户可以优先查看最新工作日志，也可以直接在页面上删除该 Factory。</p>

<p lang="en">A UI change contributed by @neubig groups sub-agent conversations under their parent conversation, making delegated work easier to follow. Changes contributed by @jpshackelford let users see the latest Factory worklogs first and delete a Factory from its own page.</p>
