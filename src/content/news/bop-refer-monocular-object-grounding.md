<p lang="zh-CN">Tomas Hodan 等人于 2026 年 11 月 25 日向 arXiv 提交 BOP-Refer 预印本。基准从 9 个 BOP 数据集选取日常和工业物体图像，为图像配上由视觉语言模型生成、经人工核验的指代表达，再利用物体的 3D 模型和六自由度位姿标注推导 2D 与 3D 参考框。这样可以用同一张图和同一段描述，分别检验模型能否在图像中找到目标，以及能否在三维空间中定位它。</p>

<p lang="en">Tomas Hodan and colleagues submitted the BOP-Refer preprint to arXiv on November 25, 2026. The benchmark draws images of everyday and industrial objects from nine BOP datasets. It pairs those images with referring expressions generated by vision-language models and verified by humans, then derives reference 2D and 3D boxes from object models and six-degree-of-freedom pose annotations. The same image and description can therefore test both whether a model finds the object in the image and whether it locates it in three-dimensional space.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">在作者评估的 17 个模型中，表现最好的 Gemini 4 Carbon 在该基准上的 2D AP 为 72.6%，3D AP 为 12.4%。两项指标使用不同的 IoU 阈值，其中 3D 阈值更宽松。作者发现，预测框投影到图像后通常对准目标，但深度偏差经常超过物体本身的尺寸，并将实际尺度估计列为主要误差来源。对需要从单张图像估计物体位置的团队，这组结果说明了为什么应单独评估深度和尺度。<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34862">arXiv 预印本</a></p>

<p lang="en">Among the 17 models the authors evaluated, the top performer, Gemini 4 Carbon, scored 72.6% AP in 2D and 12.4% AP in 3D on this benchmark. The two scores use different IoU thresholds, with more lenient thresholds in 3D. The authors found that predicted boxes generally lined up with the target in the image, while depth errors often exceeded the size of the object. They identified metric scale estimation as the main source of error. For teams estimating object positions from a single image, the results show why depth and scale warrant separate evaluation. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34862">arXiv preprint</a></p>
