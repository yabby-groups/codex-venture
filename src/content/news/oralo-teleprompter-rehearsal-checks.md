<p lang="zh-CN">做团队汇报时，临场多解释一句，固定速度的提词器就可能滑过下一句。Liwen Chen 转做管理后遇到了这个问题，于是做了 Oralo，用设备端语音识别把正在说的话与准备好的讲稿匹配，并高亮当前进度。<a href="https://dev.to/liwen_chen/i-moved-from-coding-to-management-then-i-built-a-teleprompter-to-find-my-next-line-18ph">Liwen Chen 的开发记录</a></p>

<p lang="en">Add an explanation during a team update and a fixed-speed teleprompter may scroll past your next line. Liwen Chen ran into this after moving into management and built Oralo, which uses on-device speech recognition to match what the speaker says against a prepared script and highlight their progress. <a href="https://dev.to/liwen_chen/i-moved-from-coding-to-management-then-i-built-a-teleprompter-to-find-my-next-line-18ph">Liwen Chen’s development account</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">按 Chen 的描述，匹配成功时高亮推进，停顿或匹配不确定时保持原位。讲完题外话后，重新念到有辨识度的句子，可以给它新的定位线索。彩排时用下一场演示的实际讲稿，记录恢复时是否还得低头找稿。试读流程依据作者自述设计，本站未运行 Oralo。</p>

<p lang="en">Chen describes the highlight advancing when speech matches the script and holding during pauses or uncertain matches. After an aside, a distinctive line can give it a fresh cue. Rehearse with the script for your next presentation and record whether you still have to look down to find your place. The rehearsal checks draw on Chen’s account. Codex Venture did not run Oralo.</p>
