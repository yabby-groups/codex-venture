<p lang="zh-CN">做角色试装时，可以先选一个适合展示服装轮廓的姿势，再加入想看的衣服，最后决定是否指定角色的脸。Mirra 的项目文档将姿势列为唯一必填项。它的图库提供无脸灰色人偶姿势、无人穿着的服装图，以及采用 CC0 的 AI 生成人脸。</p>

<p lang="en">For a character costume study, start with a pose that shows the outline of the garment, add the outfit you want to see, and then decide whether to specify the character’s face. Mirra’s project documentation lists pose as the only required input. Its galleries offer faceless gray mannequin poses, garments shown without a wearer, and AI-generated faces under CC0.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">要比较两套服装，可以保留相同的脸和姿势参考，只更换服装输入。这个做法控制的是输入。逐张检查成图中的面部特征和身体姿态，才能判断两张图是否足够一致，适合放在一起评审。<a href="https://github.com/yldm-ai/mirra">Mirra 官方仓库</a></p>

<p lang="en">To compare two outfits, keep the same face and pose references and change the garment input. That controls what goes into each generation. Check the resulting facial features and body position in both images before using them for a side-by-side review. <a href="https://github.com/yldm-ai/mirra">Official Mirra repository</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">资料核对日期为 2026 年 11 月 28 日。项目称同一套 Go 代码可构建到 macOS、iOS、Android 和 Windows。MIT 许可证适用于代码，图库图片各有适用条款。本文依据项目文档，换装前后的生成一致性待实测。</p>

<p lang="en">Documentation reviewed November 28, 2026. The project says its Go codebase builds for macOS, iOS, Android, and Windows. The MIT license covers the code, while gallery images have their own terms. This write-up is based on project documentation. Consistency across outfit changes still needs hands-on verification.</p>
