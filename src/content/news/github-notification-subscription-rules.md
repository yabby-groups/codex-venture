<p lang="zh-CN">GitHub 将从 2026 年 12 月 1 日起，用约一周时间分批调整 GitHub.com 的通知订阅规则。手动退订后，即使你回来留言、提交拉取请求审查，或遇到线程状态变更和合并队列活动，也不会因此恢复订阅。有人提及你、给你分配任务或请求你审查拉取请求时，仍会触发重新订阅。</p>

<p lang="en">GitHub will roll out new notification subscription rules on GitHub.com over about a week starting December 1, 2026. After you manually unsubscribe, commenting again or submitting a pull request review will leave you unsubscribed, as will thread state changes and merge queue activity. A direct mention, an assignment, or a pull request review request will still resubscribe you.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">团队提及也会随当前成员身份和提及状态更新。如果团队提及仍在，后来加入团队的成员会收到该线程后续活动的通知。如果团队提及被移除，或你离开团队，基于该提及的通知就会停止。若你还因留言或被分配任务而订阅该线程，移除提及后仍会收到通知。</p>

<p lang="en">Team mention notifications will follow current team membership and whether the mention is still present. If you join a team after it was mentioned and the mention remains, you’ll get notifications for future activity in that thread. Removing the team mention or leaving the team stops notifications based on that mention. If you’re also subscribed because you commented or were assigned, removing the mention leaves that subscription in place.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">迁移期间，不同账户可能会暂时看到不同的通知行为。GitHub 表示用户无需操作，GitHub Enterprise Server 不在本次调整范围内。<a href="https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-24-more-predictable-notifications-on-github-com">GitHub 官方更新日志</a></p>

<p lang="en">Notification behavior may vary by account during the rollout. GitHub says users don’t need to take any action and the rollout applies only to GitHub.com. <a href="https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-24-more-predictable-notifications-on-github-com">Official GitHub changelog</a></p>
