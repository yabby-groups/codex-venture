<p lang="zh-CN">Cloudflare 于 11 月 25 日推出 Search for Agents，把原本需要手动配置的站内搜索接入集中到一个开关。Celso Martinho、Gabriel Massadas 和 Nelson Duarte 在官方公告中介绍，站点管理员可以在对应站点的控制台 Quick Actions 中开启，也可以通过 API 启用。<a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/search-for-agents">Cloudflare 官方公告</a></p>

<p lang="en">Cloudflare launched Search for Agents on November 25, bringing a previously manual site search setup behind a single toggle. In the announcement, Celso Martinho, Gabriel Massadas, and Nelson Duarte say site administrators can enable it under Quick Actions in their zone dashboard or through the API. <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/search-for-agents">Cloudflare announcement</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">启用后，Cloudflare 会自动创建 AI Search 实例，开始抓取并索引站点内容，并在站点自己的域名下提供可被 AI 智能体发现和调用的搜索 API。所有 Cloudflare 客户均可使用，免费套餐有用量限制。<a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/search-for-agents">功能与可用范围</a></p>

<p lang="en">Once enabled, Cloudflare creates an AI Search instance, starts crawling and indexing the site’s content, and exposes a search API on the site’s own domain that AI agents can discover and use. The feature is available to all Cloudflare customers, with usage limits on the Free plan. <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/search-for-agents">Feature and availability details</a></p>
