<h2><span lang="zh-CN">让每个工具跑完同一个日常任务</span><span lang="en">Give every tool the same day-to-day task</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">下面是一份可自行执行的试用方案。选一批团队有权用于试用的样本，给各候选工具相同的输入、待查问题和交付要求。例如，让团队成员找出一类错误、保存支持判断的案例，再更新一条评估规则并重跑。试用前明确由谁执行、谁复核，以及结果应如何交给下一位同事。</p>

<p lang="en">For your own trial, pick a set of examples your team is authorized to use and give each candidate the same inputs, question, and deliverable. For example, ask team members to identify one kind of error, save the cases that support their finding, then change one evaluation rule and rerun it. Decide up front who does the work, who reviews it, and how the results are handed off.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">每轮保留同一份记录，写下实际完成的步骤、需要额外帮助的环节、重跑前后的结果和复核者遇到的障碍。附上试用日期、工具版本或套餐，以及可供团队检查的输出。如果某轮未能复现结果，记录它停在哪一步，再判断阻碍是否会影响日常工作。2025 年的课程记录提供了试用设计的依据，具体选型结论来自团队当次试用。</p>

<p lang="en">Keep the same record for each run. Note the steps completed, where someone needed help, what the rerun produced, and what slowed down the reviewer. Add the trial date, tool version or plan, and outputs the team can inspect. If a result can’t be reproduced, document where the run stopped and decide whether that obstacle would matter in daily work. The 2025 course record supplies the trial design, while the team’s own run supplies the selection evidence.</p>
