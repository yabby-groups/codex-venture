<p lang="zh-CN">微软于 2026 年 11 月 24 日在 Ignite 公布 Foundry 更新。Foundry 博客上，开发者体验首席产品经理 Nick Brady 介绍，Insights 和多轮评估已正式可用。维护智能体的团队可以用 Insights 从运行轨迹中找出反复出现的问题，再用多轮评估检查问题在后续对话中是否重现。</p>

<p lang="en">Microsoft announced its Foundry updates at Ignite on November 24, 2026. In the Foundry blog, Nick Brady, principal product manager for developer experience, said Insights and multi-turn evaluation are now generally available. Teams maintaining agents can use Insights to spot recurring issues in traces and use multi-turn evaluation to check whether those issues recur across a conversation.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">连接 Insights 和 Agent Optimizer 的新流程仍处于预览阶段。按微软的说明，团队可以从真实对话中提炼可复用的技能，也可以寻找达到自身质量门槛、成本更低的配置，由团队决定哪些改动进入下一版。人工标注和评分量表评估器也处于预览阶段。</p>

<p lang="en">The new workflow connecting Insights to Agent Optimizer is in preview. Microsoft says teams can learn reusable skills from real conversations and look for lower-cost configurations that still meet their quality threshold. Teams decide which changes go into the next version. Human annotation and rubric evaluators are also in preview.</p>
