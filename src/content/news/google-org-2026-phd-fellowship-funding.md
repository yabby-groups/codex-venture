<p lang="zh-CN">Google.org 科学进展总监 Leslie Yeh 于 2026 年 11 月 12 日宣布，Google.org 将为 2026 年度 Google 博士生奖学金计划提供 1300 万美元。入选博士生将获得研究经费、算力资源，以及与研究同行交流的机会。<a href="https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/google-org/phd-fellows-2026">Google 官方公告</a></p>

<p lang="en">On November 12, 2026, Leslie Yeh, Google.org’s director of scientific progress, announced that Google.org would provide $13 million for the 2026 Google PhD Fellowship program. Fellows will receive research funding, access to computing resources, and opportunities to connect with other researchers. <a href="https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/google-org/phd-fellows-2026">Google’s announcement</a></p>
