<p lang="zh-CN">Cline 在 11 月 26 日发布的 v4.1.28 中更新了模型目录。OpenAI 提供商的列表移除了上游标记为弃用的模型 ID，包括 GPT-4o、GPT-4.1、GPT-5、GPT-5.3-Codex 和 o3。ChatGPT 订阅对应的列表也移除了 GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark。</p>

<p lang="en">Cline updated its model catalog in v4.1.28, released November 26. The OpenAI provider’s list dropped model IDs marked deprecated upstream, including GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, GPT-5, GPT-5.3-Codex, and o3. The list for ChatGPT subscriptions also dropped GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark.</p>

<p lang="zh-CN">同一版本新增 Concentrate 和 Prima Labs 两个提供商选项。对于依赖特定模型的用户，发布说明确认的变化范围是 Cline 的模型目录。</p>

<p lang="en">The release also adds Concentrate and Prima Labs as provider options. For users who rely on a specific model, the release notes confirm changes to what Cline lists in its catalog.</p>
