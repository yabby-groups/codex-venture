<h2><span lang="zh-CN">Microsoft Foundry 正式推出多轮评估</span><span lang="en">Microsoft Foundry makes multi-turn evaluation generally available</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">微软 Foundry 的 Nick Brady 在 11 月 24 日发布的 Ignite 更新中宣布，Insights 和多轮评估已正式可用。团队可以评估智能体在多轮对话中的表现。将 Insights 中反复出现的问题交给 Agent Optimizer 生成可测试的改动，仍属于预览功能，团队可自行决定哪些改动进入下一版。<a href="https://devblogs.microsoft.com/foundry/whats-new-in-microsoft-foundry-ignite-2026">微软 Foundry 公告</a></p>

<p lang="en">In Microsoft Foundry’s November 24 Ignite update, Nick Brady announced that Insights and multi-turn evaluation are generally available. Teams can assess how agents perform across a conversation. The workflow that takes recurring issues from Insights to Agent Optimizer for testable changes is in preview, and teams decide which changes go into the next version. <a href="https://devblogs.microsoft.com/foundry/whats-new-in-microsoft-foundry-ignite-2026">Microsoft Foundry announcement</a></p>

<h2><span lang="zh-CN">LiteLLM 为 OpenSearch 直通请求增加按密钥读写授权</span><span lang="en">LiteLLM adds per-key read and write grants for OpenSearch passthrough</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">11 月 25 日发布的 LiteLLM v1.111.0 支持通过代理向 OpenSearch 索引转发请求，并可按密钥分别授予读取或写入权限。代理管理员可以为只需读取索引的客户端发放仅有读取权限的密钥。<a href="https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/releases/tag/v1.111.0">LiteLLM 发布记录</a></p>

<p lang="en">LiteLLM v1.111.0, released November 25, adds proxy passthrough for OpenSearch indexes with separate read and write grants per key. Proxy administrators can issue read-only keys to clients that only need to read an index. <a href="https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/releases/tag/v1.111.0">LiteLLM release notes</a></p>

<h2><span lang="zh-CN">GitHub.com 将调整手动取消订阅后的通知规则</span><span lang="en">GitHub.com changes what can resubscribe you to a thread</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">GitHub.com 将从 12 月 1 日起用约一周时间逐步调整通知规则。手动取消订阅后，发表评论或提交拉取请求评审等常规操作将保留退订状态。被提及、被指派或收到评审请求时，系统仍会重新订阅。适用范围为 GitHub.com。<a href="https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-24-more-predictable-notifications-on-github-com">GitHub 更新日志</a></p>

<p lang="en">GitHub.com will roll out revised notification rules over about a week starting December 1. After you manually unsubscribe, routine actions such as commenting or submitting a pull request review will leave you unsubscribed. A mention, assignment, or review request will still resubscribe you. The rollout applies to GitHub.com. <a href="https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-24-more-predictable-notifications-on-github-com">GitHub changelog</a></p>

<h2><span lang="zh-CN">六模型预印本发现，4 位量化方案需按格式评估</span><span lang="en">Six-model preprint finds that 4-bit quantization recipes depend on the format</span></h2>

<p lang="zh-CN">Natalia Frumkin 等人在 11 月 24 日提交的预印本中，用六个大语言模型评估了三种微缩放格式。作者发现，Hadamard 旋转对 MXFP4 的帮助比对 NVFP4 更明显，新提出的免校准缩放策略 Midmax 则提高了多数受测 MXFP4 配置的准确率。对部署团队而言，从一种 4 位格式迁移到另一种时，原有的量化方案需要重新评估。<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32873">arXiv 预印本</a></p>

<p lang="en">In a preprint submitted November 24, Natalia Frumkin and colleagues evaluated three microscaling formats across six language models. The authors found that Hadamard rotation helped MXFP4 more than NVFP4, and that Midmax, their calibration-free scale selection strategy, improved accuracy in most tested MXFP4 configurations. For deployment teams, switching between the two 4-bit formats calls for reevaluating the quantization recipe. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32873">arXiv preprint</a></p>
