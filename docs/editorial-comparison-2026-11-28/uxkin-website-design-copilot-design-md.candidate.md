<p lang="zh-CN">当一个网站从首页扩展到定价页时，放在项目文件里的视觉方向可以供两页共同参考。按 UXKIN 在 Open VSX 上的介绍，Website Design 会参考真实网站，把设计方向写入 <code>DESIGN.md</code>。发布者还把响应式建页和页面审查列为扩展用途。<a href="https://open-vsx.org/api/uxkin/website-design">Open VSX 扩展记录</a></p>

<p lang="en">When a site grows from a homepage to a pricing page, both pages can refer to a visual direction kept in a project file. In its Open VSX listing, UXKIN describes Website Design as drawing on real websites and recording the design direction in <code>DESIGN.md</code>. The publisher also lists responsive page building and page review among the extension’s uses. <a href="https://open-vsx.org/api/uxkin/website-design">Open VSX extension record</a></p>

<p lang="zh-CN">登记记录显示，1.0.0 版于 2026 年 11 月 1 日发布，声明的 VS Code 引擎范围为 <code>^1.96.0</code>。这些用途来自发布者的目录描述，实际操作流程和页面效果仍待验证。<a href="https://open-vsx.org/api/uxkin/website-design">Open VSX 版本与兼容性元数据</a></p>

<p lang="en">The registry dates version 1.0.0 to November 1, 2026, and lists a VS Code engine range of <code>^1.96.0</code>. The stated uses come from the publisher’s listing. The workflow and resulting pages remain unverified. <a href="https://open-vsx.org/api/uxkin/website-design">Open VSX version and compatibility metadata</a></p>
